 ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze coffee shop sales, orders, and quantity sold across different time periods, store locations, and product categories. This report empowers business stakeholders to track key performance indicators (KPIs), uncover trends, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📌 Business Objectives

To monitor and optimize the performance of coffee shops through:

- Monthly sales trends
- Store-level sales analysis
- Product and category performance insights
- Time-based sales patterns (day, hour, weekday/weekend)

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

### ✅ Sales KPIs:
- **Total Sales** per month
- Month-over-Month (MoM) sales trends
- Difference from previous month

### ✅ Orders KPIs:
- **Total Orders** per month
- MoM growth or decline
- Previous month comparison

### ✅ Quantity KPIs:
- **Total Quantity Sold** per month
- Quantity trends and changes compared to previous month

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 Calendar Heat Map
- Displays daily sales using a color-coded calendar.
- Darker shades = higher sales volume.
- Tooltips show Sales, Orders, and Quantity for each day.

### 🔹 Weekday vs. Weekend Analysis
- Compares sales patterns on weekdays vs. weekends.
- Identifies consumer behavior differences across time.

### 🔹 Store Location Analysis
- Compares sales by different store branches.
- Highlights Month-over-Month performance changes.

### 🔹 Daily Sales Line Chart with Average Line
- Shows daily sales within the selected month.
- Includes an average sales line.
- Highlights exceptional sales days (above/below average).

### 🔹 Product Category & Top Products
- Visual breakdown of sales by product category.
- Identifies **Top 10 best-selling products**.

### 🔹 Sales by Day and Hour Heat Map
- Analyzes performance by hour of day and day of week.
- Tooltips provide precise metrics on hover.
## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `CoffeeShopDashboard.pbix` in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use slicers to explore data by month, store, and category.
4. Hover over visuals to explore tooltips and patterns.
5. Use filters and bookmarks to customize your view.
